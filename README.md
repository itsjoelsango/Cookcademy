## Description

Cookcademy is an iOS recipe app built with Swift using the SwiftUI framework. Designed with both novice cooks and culinary experts in mind, the app provides a seamless and engaging platform to discover, create, and manage your favorite recipes. With categories tailored for every meal of the day — **breakfast**, **lunch**, **dinner**, and **dessert** — Cookcademy ensures that you'll always have the perfect recipe at your fingertips.

## Features

- **Rich Recipe Collection:** Browse a wide array of recipes spanning four distinct categories: breakfast, lunch, dinner, and dessert.
- **SwiftUI Interface:** Enjoy a modern, fluid, and responsive design built entirely with SwiftUI.
- **Recipe Management:** Easily create, edit, and delete recipes directly within the app.
- **Interactive Cooking Guides:** Follow detailed, step-by-step instructions with images and optional video support.
- **Search & Filtering:** Quickly locate recipes by ingredient, cuisine, dietary preference, or cooking time.

## Next upgrade features 
- **Community Sharing:** Share your recipes with a growing community, rate others’ recipes, and exchange culinary tips.
- **Meal Planning & Shopping Lists:** Organize your weekly meals and automatically generate shopping lists tailored to your recipes.

## Installation

### Prerequisites

- **Xcode 14 or later** (Xcode 14+ is recommended for the latest SwiftUI capabilities)
- **iOS 16 or higher** (to run the app on an iOS device or simulator)

### Getting Started

1. **Clone the Repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/itsjoelsango/Cookcademy.git
   cd cookcademy

## Open the Project in Xcode

1. **Open the Project in Xcode:**
   - Locate and double-click the `Cookcademy.xcodeproj` file in the project directory to open it in Xcode.

2. **Build and Run:**
   - Select your preferred simulator or connect your iOS device.
   - Click the Run button (or press Command + R) to build and launch the app.

## Usage

1. **Home Screen:**
   - Discover featured recipes and explore the various meal categories at a glance.
2. **Search Functionality:**
   - Use the search bar to find recipes by keyword, ingredient, or cuisine.
3. **Recipe Detail View:**
   - Tap any recipe to view comprehensive details including ingredients, step-by-step cooking directions, timers, and nutritional facts.
4. **Submit a Recipe:**
   - Add your own recipes through the "Submit Recipe" feature, complete with photos and proper categorization.

### Upcoming Usage features
1. **User Profile:**
   - Log in or create an account to manage your recipes, favorites, and personal settings.
2. **Meal Planner:**
   - Organize your weekly meals with an integrated planner that automatically creates shopping lists based on your selected recipes.

## Recipe Categories

Cookcademy keeps your meals varied and well-planned by organizing recipes into four primary categories:

- **Breakfast:** Start your day with nutritious and energizing recipes.
- **Lunch:** Enjoy an assortment of midday meals, whether for a quick bite or a relaxed lunch break.
- **Dinner:** Delight in hearty recipes for dinner, from comforting classics to innovative creations.
- **Dessert:** Indulge your sweet tooth with a selection of delicious dessert recipes.
